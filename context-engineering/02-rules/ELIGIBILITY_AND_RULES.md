# Eligibility and Rules Digest

> This is a practical digest, not legal advice. The official rules control.

## Eligibility

The event is open to:

- individuals at least the age of majority where they reside;
- teams composed of eligible individuals;
- legally existing organizations, including companies and nonprofits.

An eligible individual may enter alone, join multiple teams/organizations, and submit separately as an individual. Teams and organizations must appoint an authorized representative.

## Exclusions

The rules exclude people or organizations where US or local law prohibits participation or prize receipt, including named examples Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and OFAC-designated jurisdictions. They also exclude:

- Sponsor/Administrator and other promotion entities;
- their employees, representatives, agents, specified family/household members, parents, subsidiaries, and affiliates;
- judges and companies or individuals employing judges;
- people involved in design, production, promotion, execution, or distribution;
- actual or apparent conflicts of interest.

## Nigeria assessment

**[OFFICIAL]** Nigeria is not named in the event's exclusions.  
**[OBSERVED]** The checked OFAC sanctions-program page did not list Nigeria as a country-wide sanctions program. OFAC still maintains person/entity-specific programs, so this is not blanket clearance for every individual or organization.  
**[INFERENCE]** An adult resident of Nigeria appears eligible to submit remotely, subject to local law, sanctions screening, service access, and final Sponsor/Administrator verification.

Prize receipt is operationally plausible because rules allow electronic payment to a winner's bank account. Non-US winners may need W-8BEN. Winners bear bank, exchange, tax, and local reporting obligations. No source promises that every Nigerian bank or payment route will work, so prize delivery is not guaranteed in advance.

## Project rules

- Build a working generative-AI media software application using **both** B2 and Genblaze.
- Solve a real user problem and show a path to production.
- App must install/run consistently on its intended platform and work as depicted.
- Existing projects are allowed only if they add B2 and Genblaze after the event began and clearly explain the significant update.
- Third-party SDKs, APIs, data, media, trademarks, music, and assets require appropriate authorization/licensing.
- Open source is allowed if licenses are followed and the entry materially builds on/enhances it.
- The submission must be original, entrant-owned work and not infringe third-party rights.
- Third-party technical help is allowed only while the submission remains the entrant's work product and ideas.
- Projects developed with financial or preferential support from Backblaze/Devpost may be disqualified for conflict.

## Entry mechanics

1. Register on Devpost.
2. Create a Backblaze B2 account.
3. Use the Genblaze repository/SDK.
4. Build during the period.
5. Complete all submission fields before the deadline.

## Multiple submissions

Allowed, but each entry must be unique and substantially different in the Sponsor/Devpost's judgment.

## Access and testing

- Provide a functioning app link.
- Keep it free and unrestricted for judging through the end of judging.
- If authentication is required, include test credentials and clear instructions.
- Judges may choose not to test and can judge from text/images/video alone.
- For proprietary hardware not widely available, organizers may request physical access. Smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers are explicitly distinguished from this concern. A normal web/desktop app does not create a stated travel requirement.

## Language

All materials must be English or include English translations, including demo, description, and test instructions.

## Submission changes

Drafts may be edited before the deadline. After the period ends, the competition submission cannot be altered, though the personal Devpost portfolio project can be updated. Only limited organizer-approved IP/privacy/inappropriate-content corrections may be accepted afterward.

## IP and publicity

Entrants retain IP. Backblaze receives a non-exclusive license for judging. Backblaze and Devpost may promote the submission and contributors' names/likeness/voice/image during the event and for three years afterward. Some submission components may be public; others may be limited to judges and administrators.

## Rule precedence and changes

Official rules prevail over conflicting event materials. Rules can change. Ambiguities should be raised in writing before the deadline.
