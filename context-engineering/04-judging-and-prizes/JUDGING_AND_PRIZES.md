# Judging and Prizes

## Two-stage evaluation

### Stage 1 — pass/fail viability gate

The entry must reasonably fit the theme and apply the required APIs/SDKs. A polished app that uses only B2 or only Genblaze can fail before scoring.

### Stage 2 — equally weighted criteria

Each criterion is effectively 25%:

1. **Real-World Utility** — practical problem, clear audience, actual desirability.
2. **Production Readiness** — reliability and support for real workflows beyond a demo.
3. **B2 Storage + Data Orchestration** — meaningful storage, organization, serving, or management of media, metadata, provenance, or assets.
4. **Use of Genblaze** — meaningful model/provider/step orchestration.

## Tie-break order

Ties are compared in the listed criterion order:

1. Real-World Utility
2. Production Readiness
3. B2 Storage + Data Orchestration
4. Use of Genblaze
5. Judge vote if still tied

This makes utility the highest-priority tie-break signal even though all criteria are equally weighted.

## Feedback prize

- 10 awards.
- One hour of mentorship/architecture guidance from Backblaze engineers.
- Available to eligible submissions that provide product feedback.
- Evaluated on completeness, viability, and potential impact.
- One entrant may win one overall prize and one feedback prize.

## Prize administration

Potential winners must pass identity/qualification/contribution verification and return required forms within 10 business days after they are sent. Monetary awards may be mailed or electronically sent and should arrive within 60 days after completed forms are received. Winners bear fees, exchange costs, taxes, foreign-exchange compliance, and reporting. Non-US residents may need W-8BEN; withholding may apply.

## Practical scoring evidence

| Criterion | Strong evidence |
|---|---|
| Utility | named user, painful job, before/after, complete workflow, immediate outcome |
| Production | deployed app, health check, retries/fallbacks, input validation, tests, observability, predictable degraded mode |
| B2 | structured object layout, intermediate + final assets, metadata/manifests, durable links, lifecycle or Object Lock where justified, retrieval/search/indexing |
| Genblaze | multiple meaningful steps, chain/fan-out/agent loop, provider fallback, streaming, manifest lineage, real verification |
