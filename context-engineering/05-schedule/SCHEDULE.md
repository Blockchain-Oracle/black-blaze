## Source discrepancy

The Devpost schedule page displayed a submission start of **June 22 at 12:30 PM EDT**, while the official rules state **10:00 AM ET**. The rules expressly control in a conflict. The closing deadline is consistent across sources.

Do not aim for the legal cutoff. Recommended internal submission target:

- **Feature freeze:** Aug 1, 10:00 PM WAT
- **Final deployment and evidence run:** Aug 2
- **Video/publication and Devpost draft:** Aug 3 morning
- **Submit:** no later than **Aug 3, 4:00 PM WAT**
- **Contingency buffer:** 6 hours

## Availability obligation

Keep the app, media, repository access, and judge test account operational until after **Aug 11, 10:00 PM WAT**, preferably through winner announcement.
