# Executive Brief

## The actual challenge

Build a **working generative AI media application** that uses **Backblaze B2 Cloud Storage** and **Genblaze**. The organizer is not looking for a thin model wrapper. Official language repeatedly emphasizes:

- a clear real-world user problem;
- production-shaped workflows;
- reliable generation and error handling;
- durable media and metadata storage;
- meaningful pipeline orchestration;
- provenance and verifiable outputs;
- a usable experience.

## The winning shape

The best evidence-backed interpretation is a focused product in which:

1. a real user completes a recognizable job;
2. Genblaze performs a multi-step, multi-model/provider, fallback, evaluation, or lineage-bearing media workflow;
3. B2 is part of the product's architecture—not just a final upload destination;
4. failures, retries, storage organization, and provenance are visible and testable;
5. the whole value proposition is understandable in the first 20–30 seconds of the demo.

The official Backblaze blog says "the pipeline is becoming the moat" and calls out streaming progress, fan-out, provider fallbacks, low latency, durable storage, and proof of generation. Treat this as strategic sponsor intent, while remembering the four published judging criteria control scoring.
